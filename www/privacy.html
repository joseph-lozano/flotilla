Privacy

Flotilla has no accounts, and currently no trackers or analytics. If any are added in the future, they will be opt-in.

Your threads, keys and files stay on your devices and your own server.

The services you connect, such as boat.dev, GitHub and your model providers, get what you send them, under their own privacy policies.

If your server uses Flotilla's push relay, which runs on Cloudflare, the relay keeps your phone's notification token, a random id with a hashed secret, and when it was registered and last used. It can't read your notifications. It deletes them when you unpair, when Apple reports the app gone, or after 90 days unused (Cloudflare can restore deleted data for 30 days).
